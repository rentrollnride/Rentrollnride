# VEHICLE RENTAL AGREEMENT AND POLICY ACKNOWLEDGMENT

This Vehicle Rental Agreement ("Agreement") is between **Rent Ride Roll LLC** ("Company") and the renter identified below ("Renter").

## Rental information

**Renter:** [RenterName]  
**Email:** [RenterEmail]  
**Phone:** [RenterPhone]  
**Vehicle:** [Vehicle]  
**Pickup:** [Pickup]  
**Scheduled Return:** [Return]  
**Rental Rate:** [Rate]  
**Refundable Deposit:** [Deposit]  
**Approved Travel Area:** [ApprovedTravelArea]

The rental remains subject to final identity, license, insurance, payment-card, and vehicle-condition verification at pickup.

## 1. Driver eligibility and authorized drivers

Renter must present a valid driver's license, current proof of insurance acceptable to the Company, and an accepted debit or credit card before taking possession of the vehicle. Renters age 21 and older meet the standard age requirement. Renters ages 18 through 20 may be subject to an under-age fee disclosed before pickup.

Only drivers expressly approved by the Company may operate the vehicle. Renter remains responsible for the vehicle and for the conduct of every authorized driver during the rental period.

## 2. Payment and refundable deposit

Rental charges are due as agreed with the Company. A refundable deposit of [Deposit] is collected at pickup. The deposit may be applied, to the extent permitted by law and this Agreement, toward unpaid rental charges, tolls, citations, cleaning or smoking charges, damage, fuel, late-return charges, or other amounts properly due under this Agreement. Any remaining refundable balance will be handled according to the Company's stated return process.

## 3. Tickets, citations, parking charges, and tolls

Renter is responsible for all tolls, parking charges, traffic or camera citations, impound charges, administrative charges, and similar costs arising from use or possession of the vehicle during the rental period, except to the extent caused solely by the Company before the rental began.

If the Company receives a notice or invoice relating to the rental period, Renter authorizes the Company to seek reimbursement for the underlying charge and any disclosed administrative cost permitted by law.

## 4. Smoking, vaping, and cleaning

Smoking and vaping are prohibited in the vehicle. Renter is responsible for reasonable cleaning, deodorizing, remediation, or restoration costs caused by smoking, vaping, excessive dirt, stains, odors, biohazards, pet-related damage, or other conditions beyond ordinary use, to the extent permitted by law and disclosed by the Company.

## 5. Vehicle condition and damage

Renter must return the vehicle in substantially the same condition in which it was received, ordinary wear excepted. Renter is responsible, subject to applicable law and insurance coverage, for loss of or damage to the vehicle occurring during the rental period, including collision damage, vandalism, theft, misuse, interior damage, tire or wheel damage, towing, storage, and reasonable related costs.

Renter must promptly report any accident, theft, vandalism, warning light, mechanical issue, or material damage to the Company and cooperate with reasonable incident and insurance documentation.

## 6. Prohibited conduct, weapons, drugs, and illegal substances

The vehicle may not be used to transport, possess, conceal, manufacture, distribute, or facilitate the use or sale of illegal drugs, controlled substances, stolen property, or other illegal contraband.

The vehicle may not be used in connection with criminal activity or any unlawful purpose.

Weapons may not be carried, stored, or transported in the vehicle in violation of applicable law or Company policy. Renter is responsible for complying with all federal, state, and local laws concerning possession and transportation of any lawful weapon.

## 7. Approved travel area

Unless the Company gives prior written approval, the vehicle may be operated only within:

**[ApprovedTravelArea]**

Travel outside the approved area requires advance approval from the Company and may result in additional disclosed charges, mileage terms, or other conditions. Unauthorized travel outside the approved area may constitute a breach of this Agreement.

## 8. Prohibited vehicle use

The vehicle may not be:
- operated by an unauthorized or unlicensed driver;
- used while the driver is impaired by alcohol, drugs, or any substance that makes driving unsafe;
- used for racing, speed testing, towing, pushing another vehicle, off-road use inconsistent with the vehicle's intended use, or other abusive operation;
- overloaded beyond legal or manufacturer limits;
- subleased, re-rented, or used for hire unless the Company gives written approval;
- intentionally driven in a manner that creates unreasonable risk of damage or loss.

## 9. Fuel, mileage, late return, and extensions

Renter must comply with the fuel, mileage, pickup, and return terms disclosed for the rental. The vehicle must be returned by the scheduled return time unless the Company approves an extension.

Extensions are not effective until approved by the Company. Renter is responsible for any properly disclosed late-return, excess-mileage, refueling, or related charges permitted under the Agreement and applicable law.

## 10. Insurance and responsibility

Renter must maintain the insurance required by the Company for the rental. Renter is responsible for confirming whether the Renter's personal insurance or other coverage applies to the rented vehicle.

Nothing in this Agreement expands or reduces insurance coverage beyond the applicable policy terms or applicable law.

## 11. Accidents, breakdowns, and law enforcement

Renter must promptly notify the Company of any collision, theft, impoundment, police contact involving the vehicle, or significant mechanical problem. Renter must not abandon the vehicle or authorize repairs, towing, or other material work without Company approval except where immediate action is reasonably necessary for safety or required by law.

## 12. No transfer of agreement

Renter may not assign, transfer, sublease, or re-rent the vehicle or this Agreement without the Company's prior written approval.

## 13. Electronic records and signature

Renter agrees to receive this Agreement and related notices electronically and agrees that an electronic signature has the same intent and effect as a handwritten signature to the extent permitted by applicable law.

## 14. Entire agreement and controlling terms

This document, together with any vehicle-condition report, rate disclosure, approved extension, and other written rental terms provided by the Company, constitutes the rental agreement between the parties. If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain in effect to the extent permitted by law.
